# 2D stages: what Evan's games already do (M7.1)

How afterglass, 2060td and climbing-world-traveler draw in 2D, with dirtbag's parallax, read on 2026-10-27 to design M7.1's 2D scenes. The decisions it led to are D83; the design is [specs/2d-scenes.md](../specs/2d-scenes.md). Facts carry their file and line; opinions are marked as such.

## What none of them has

- **No image files, sheets or atlases.** All four draw every picture in code: afterglass from `fillRect` at world resolution (`prototype/src/px/kit.js:1-39`); 2060td from vector glyphs and a baked terrain canvas (`game/ground.ts:44-190`); climbing-world-traveler as thick-outlined vector cartoons (`src/ui/wall/toon.ts:1-4`); dirtbag as layers painted in code. 2060td's roadmap defers an atlas pipeline (`docs/ROADMAP.md:173`).
- **So M7.1 defines the sheet format** (D83: a grid with named clips), and it comes from no game.

## afterglass: the only pixel-perfect one

- **Scale.** The world is 112 px wide (`slice/data.js:966`). `layout()` takes the largest whole scale from 1 to 6 the width allows, never below 3 for height, so a tall keep pans rather than shrinks (`page/stage.js:34-59`). The canvas is `ceil(vw/scale)+1` world pixels, enlarged by CSS with `image-rendering: pixelated` (`season.css:15-27`), smoothing off (`slice/draw.js:22-26`). It ignores `devicePixelRatio`.
- **Zoom** is kept as whole steps from the fitted scale, so turning a phone keeps it sensible (`stage.js:32-33`); a pinch rounds to whole scales (`page/input.js:208`).
- **Camera.** Eased by `1 − exp(−dt·7)` and snapped under reduced motion (`stage.js:155-168`); a drag is clamped to the castle (`89-97`); zoom keeps the point under the finger (`120-133`). The canvas is drawn at whole world pixels and a CSS translate adds the fraction, so the camera glides (`169-179`).
- **Animation** has no frames on file: a frame is `floor(t·rate + phase) mod n` on wall-clock seconds (walking 2 frames at 4 a second, a flame 3 at 9: `slice/people.js:143-147`, `kit.js:132-133`), each figure at its own phase, `t = 0` under reduced motion (`stage.js:494`). Positions are interpolated between the 10 Hz sim ticks (`page/clock.js:201-261`).
- **No parallax:** the ridges are drawn under the same camera (`draw.js:486-498`); stars, clouds, rain and snow repeat on fixed-width tiles (`500-562`).
- **The stage reads the sim and decides nothing;** art texture has its own seeded stream (`kit.js:18`) and animation "runs off the clock, so it holds no state" (`draw.js:567-568`).

## 2060td

- **Camera:** a viewport rect, a centre and a zoom with `getWorldPoint` and no cached matrix to go stale (`src/game/stage/camera.ts:13-83`). It fits the board, limits zoom to 0.95 to 6 times the fit and clamps the centre, keeping zoom relative to fit across rotation (`BoardView.ts:275-363`).
- **Not pixel art:** device pixel ratio capped at 2, the canvas at viewport × DPR (`main.ts:58-92`).
- **Interpolation** between fixed sim steps by `alpha` (`scenes/SiegeScene.ts:585-595`).
- **Draw order** by insertion; `depth` exists but nothing sorts by it (`stage/objects.ts:31, 77`).

## climbing-world-traveler

- **Parallax:** `par(f) = (anchor.x − w/2) · f`, with factors from 0.04 (a distant island) to 1 (`toon.ts:104-273`), repeated by wrapping x in screen space, laid out from a seeded stream.
- **The wall decides nothing** about the climb (`toon.ts:3-4`, `WallCanvas.tsx:27`); its step events are the stage's (`WallCanvas.tsx:38-43`).

## dirtbag

- **Parallax layers** at 0.2, 0.5 and 1.0 under a sky that stays put, each painted once wide enough never to tile (`view/paint/scenes.ts:852-931`), drawn at `−cam · p` (`view/render.ts:175-188`).
- **Logical size** 360 by 740, widening with the aspect (`view/layout.ts:13-35`).

## What M7.1 takes (opinion)

- **afterglass's scale rule,** whole scales only, a minimum, zoom as steps from the fit; and its whole-pixel camera. In three.js: a canvas of art pixels shown pixelated, textures sampled nearest, positions snapped as drawn.
- **2060td's camera** (centre and zoom, fit and clamp, zoom kept relative to the fit).
- **Parallax as dirtbag and climbing do it:** a factor per layer, a layer drawn at `camera · (1 − f)`, repeating where it must.
- **Clips on the page's clock** with a rate and a phase, held under reduced motion.
- **The stage reads; the sim decides.** All four already keep this rule.

## What doesn't carry (opinion)

- **Per-pixel drawing,** `getImageData` lighting and palette tables become textures, or shaders later.
- **CSS `image-rendering` alone** isn't enough for three.js: textures must be sampled nearest too.
- **Insertion order** as depth: M7.1 sorts by depth, keeping the file's order for equals.

## Open questions

2. Sorting by y for top-down scenes (M7.2).
